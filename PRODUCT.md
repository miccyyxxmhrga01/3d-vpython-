# Caybin Dream House

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Python, as requested. Python constructs the scene and serves a local viewer using the installed VPython WebGL engine. Small browser scripts provide the camera and controls.

## Product Purpose

Explore a modern, architecturally composed house, including its interior, by scrolling.

## Capabilities and Constraints

User confirmed warm modern architecture: concrete, timber, glass, and a pool. The model must be genuinely three dimensional and enterable. Assumed: a furnished two-storey concept home, local desktop use, no external accounts or hosting. This is a concept visualization rather than construction documentation.
